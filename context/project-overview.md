# Atlas Research v1

## Overview

Atlas Research is an AI-powered research workspace designed for software engineers. It transforms AI-assisted research into reusable engineering decisions by structuring research into persistent decision artifacts with evidence, alternatives, confidence scoring, and historical comparison.

## Goals

1. Improve decision quality and transparency.
2. Enable long-term reuse of technical research.
3. Preserve research and convert it into traceable decision-making instead of disposable answers.

## Core User Flow

1. User creates a research session.
2. User enters a technical question.
3. Atlas decomposes the query and retrieves evidence.
4. Atlas evaluates contradictions and generates a structured decision.
5. Atlas calculates confidence and stores the research.
6. User can compare this session with previous sessions.

## Features

### Research & Decisions
- Research sessions (create, reopen, continue, archive).
- Query processing and research plan generation.
- Evidence retrieval with source scoring and duplicate detection.
- Structured decision generation (context, tradeoffs, alternatives, evidence, rationale).

### Workspace & Evaluation
- Confidence engine showing evidence coverage, source quality, and risk.
- Historical comparison of outputs and decisions.
- Persistent knowledge workspace for searching and reusing research.
- Full explainability of research plans, evidence, and verification.

1. 100 active users.
2. 50 completed research sessions.
3. 30% returning users.
4. Average session completion > 70%.
